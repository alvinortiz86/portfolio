Turning Around a High-Risk Merchant of Record: From VAMP Exposure to Sub-1% Disputes
Fraud & Dispute Reduction Engagement · High-Risk Merchant of Record Platform

Situation

A startup operating as a high-risk merchant of record had grown too fast, and its fraud and dispute controls hadn't kept pace. The platform processed roughly $1B in card payments annually at a ~$1,000 average ticket, with volume projected to grow to $2B, and was already enrolled in Visa's VAMP program — with a second MID just shut down by its processor over fraud and disputes. The team was brought in to move quickly on two fronts at once: cleaning up historical fraud and disputes retroactively while standing up controls to prevent new ones.

Action

  • Enabled RDR (Rapid Dispute Resolution) with an auto-refund rule for transactions under $200 — a threshold set by analyzing the platform's own historical dispute data.
  • Enabled Visa Order Insight for dispute deflection, giving issuers transaction context before a dispute is filed.
  • Implemented stricter pre-authorization risk controls to cut fraud at the point of transaction.
  • Anticipating the approval-rate impact of tighter risk rules, enabled Network Tokens and Stripe's Auth Boost product to protect authorization rates.
  • Analyzed the resulting fraud and dispute data to block high-risk BINs and countries and add velocity rules, closing the specific patterns driving losses.

Result

  • Brought the platform's VAMP score down from 3.5% to roughly 1%, and its chargeback ratio from 2.5% to 0.60%, within three months — while protecting authorization rates throughout.
  • Estimated ~$365K in annualized savings: ~$285K from roughly 19,000 fewer chargebacks a year, plus ~$80K in avoided Visa VAMP excessive-dispute penalty fees.
  • Removed the platform's risk of processor termination — a second MID had already been shut down before this engagement began.
